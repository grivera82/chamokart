How to play

Touch controls

Drag the pad left and right to steer. Your kart speeds up on its own.
DRIFTHold while turning to hop & drift
ITEMUse your item. Hold it to drag a banana or red shell behind you as a shield, let go to use it (hold ◀◀ to throw it backwards)
◀◀Brake / reverse
❚❚Pause

Prefer a steering wheel? Settings → Steering → Tilt phone. Turn your phone sideways for the widest view, and for full screen tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Pro tips

  • Drift! Hold drift while turning. Sparks go blue → orange → purple. Let go for a mini-turbo.
  • Rocket start: hold accelerate as the 2 turns into 1. Press during the 3 and you'll stall!
  • Tricks: tap drift right after flying off a ramp for a landing boost.
  • Coins raise your top speed (max 10). Getting hit loses some.
  • Racers at the back get better items. Never give up!
